Transportation

Coming to Buenos Aires


By airplane


Aerolíneas Argentinas

Official transportation. Special discounted flights.

Destinations
Ministro Pistarini International Airport - Ezeiza (EZE), or Aeroparque Jorge Newbery (AEP).

For booking and payment choose one of the following options, mentioning the event to which you are going to participate to learn the benefits traveling with Aerolineas Argentinas.

From Argentina
  • Call Center Argentina +54 0810-222-86527, open 24 hs.
  • Offices in all Argentina.
  • Booking page.

From other countries
  • Calling to Call Center in your country of residence.
  • Offices in America, Europe and Oceania.
